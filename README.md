# 🚚 Haul Empire

A mobile-first trucking idle game. Tap the road to drive deliveries, build a fleet that hauls while you're away, catch rush orders, and push your routes from Main Street to the Galactic Ring.

**Play:** https://kurtztech.github.io/haul-empire/

- 13 vehicles, from a beat-up pickup to the Warp Courier, with ×2 milestones
- 10 routes with their own scenery and day/night cycle, ending at the Galactic Ring
- Tap combos, rush orders (cash, Tailwind, Nitro) and auto-drive
- Timed contracts with cash and dividend rewards
- IPO prestige: shares boost income forever and pay dividends for Executive Board perks
- Automation perks: Fleet Manager, Procurement, Route Planner, Rush Magnet, Back Office
- 100+ upgrades and 37 achievements
- Hold-to-buy, buy-all upgrades, time-to-afford, best-value badge, scientific notation, low-effects mode
- Offline earnings, autosave, and save export/import

Single file, no build step — just `index.html`.
